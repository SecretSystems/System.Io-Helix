What It Is

Disney+ Advertising, Explained

Disney+ is Disney's flagship streaming service, built around Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars and National Geographic programming. Since Disney introduced an ad-supported subscription tier, ad-supported Disney+ inventory has become reachable through Disney Campaign Manager where available for a given campaign — the same self-service platform used for Hulu.

Availability of Disney+ ad-supported inventory isn't guaranteed for every campaign the way Hulu inventory typically is — Secret Systems confirms what a specific campaign can actually reach at setup, rather than assuming Disney+ placement by default. Campaigns can access eligible inventory through this supported advertising platform; Secret Systems is an independent advertising service and is not an official Disney advertising partner.

How It Relates to Hulu

Same Platform, Different Inventory

Hulu

General entertainment: originals, next-day network TV, licensed movies and series. Broad, general-audience programming across genres.

Disney+

Franchise and family programming: Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, National Geographic. A more specific, brand-safe content environment that can fit a family-oriented offer particularly well.

Both run through Disney Campaign Manager, but they aren't interchangeable — a campaign's actual reach into each is confirmed at setup, not assumed from one to the other.
