What It Is

Roku Ads Manager, Explained

Roku is a connected-TV device and streaming platform, and Roku Ads Manager is its self-service advertising tool for placing video ads on The Roku Channel and ad-supported content across apps on Roku devices. Because Roku sits at the device level rather than owning a single content library the way Hulu or Paramount+ do, its ad inventory spans whatever ad-supported apps a viewer is using on their Roku device. Campaigns can access eligible inventory through this supported advertising platform; Secret Systems is an independent advertising service, not an official Roku advertising partner.

Honest Budget Note

Unlike Hulu and Paramount+, Roku does not publish a single official minimum spend figure in its own help documentation. A $500-per-campaign figure and a $20–$60 CPM range are commonly cited in third-party guides as planning estimates — treat both as estimates, not guaranteed official minimums, until confirmed directly inside Roku Ads Manager account setup.

Targeting

What Roku Campaigns Can Target

  • State, ZIP code, or DMA-level geography (one geography type per targeting rule)
  • Age and gender demographic targeting
  • Household income targeting — excluded specifically for political ad campaigns
  • Custom audience uploads through Roku Ads Manager's audience tools
  • Website-visitor retargeting via pixel, with the ability to exclude existing converters

Website Retargeting on Roku

How the Roku Pixel Works

Roku's website pixel is one of the more clearly documented retargeting tools among self-service CTV platforms. Here's the practical flow Secret Systems builds around it:

01
Pixel Install

The Roku pixel is placed on the business's website to record eligible visits.

02
Audience Build

Visitors become eligible for a retargeting audience, subject to Roku's minimum audience-size and matching rules.

03
Exclusion Logic

Visitors who already converted can be excluded, so ad spend isn't wasted re-showing an ad to an existing customer.

04
Roku Delivery

The commercial is delivered to eligible retargeting-audience members across Roku's ad-supported inventory.

05
Tracked Return

A return visit, call, or conversion is tracked back to the campaign and captured as a lead.
