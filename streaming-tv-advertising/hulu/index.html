What It Is

Hulu Advertising, Explained

Hulu is one of the most-watched ad-supported streaming services in the U.S., and it's owned and operated by Disney. Historically, buying commercial time on Hulu meant going through a direct sales relationship with Disney's ad-sales team — a process built around large national or regional brand budgets. Disney Campaign Manager changed that by opening a self-service tool that lets smaller advertisers configure, launch, and manage a Hulu campaign themselves, without the traditional agency-scale minimum commitment.

Secret Systems uses Disney Campaign Manager to plan and run Hulu campaigns on behalf of local and regional businesses. Campaigns can access eligible Hulu inventory through this supported advertising platform — Secret Systems is an independent advertising service, not an official Disney advertising partner.

Self-Service Minimum

$500 media spend / campaign

Commonly cited minimum for launching a campaign through Disney Campaign Manager's self-service tool. Media spend only — production and management are separate.

Platform

Disney Campaign Manager

Disney's official self-service tool for Hulu (and, where available, ad-supported Disney+) inventory.

Targeting

What Hulu Campaigns Can Target

Disney Campaign Manager's self-service targeting commonly includes the options below. Exact availability can change and is confirmed at campaign setup — Secret Systems doesn't promise a targeting capability a platform doesn't actually support.

  • Age & gender targeting
  • Viewer interest / genre affinity targeting
  • Household and geographic targeting (platform-dependent — confirmed per campaign)

Website-visitor retargeting and first-party audience uploads are not currently confirmed as available through Disney Campaign Manager's self-service tool for Hulu — if a campaign needs that capability, Secret Systems will confirm it directly with the platform or recommend a platform where it's documented, such as Roku Ads Manager.

Why Pair Hulu Ads With Secret Systems Instead of Running Them Alone

A Hulu commercial by itself can build brand awareness, but it doesn't answer the phone or capture a lead. Secret Systems treats the ad as one part of a system: landing page, tracking, CRM, and automated follow-up all work together so a viewer who responds becomes a tracked lead — not just an impression.
