Transparent Methodology

How the Total Is Calculated

The $2,910.36/month figure is not a rounded marketing number — it is the sum of the itemized catalog below, using each vendor's own published pricing for a five-person business. The calculator and the hero use this exact same total by default.

Per-user plans

Tools priced per seat (Zoom, Slack, Notion, DocuSign, Zendesk, and similar) are modeled at 5 users, matching the five-person business assumption.

Flat and per-channel plans

Tools priced as a flat workspace, account, or per-channel subscription (Canva, Dropbox, Shopify, QuickBooks, Buffer, and similar) are counted once, regardless of team size.

Pricing sources

Every counted price links to the vendor's own current pricing page in the catalog data. Where a vendor only publishes an annual-billed rate, the monthly-equivalent figure is used and noted.

What's excluded

Taxes, promotional discounts, and invented enterprise pricing are excluded from every figure on this page. No price was selected to hit a predetermined total.

Open source still costs something

Self-hosting is not free. Hosting, storage, backups, email delivery, payment processing, third-party APIs, AI compute, and maintenance can still carry real operating costs.

Duplicate value reviewed and excluded

SecretHelp runs on the same infrastructure as SecretSupport (both are Chatwoot-based) and is excluded from every total to avoid double-counting the same subscription twice.

Kept separate — SecretSchedule vs. SecretBookings: general meeting scheduling (Calendly) versus service-business appointment booking with staff and payment handling (Square Appointments) are different paid products.

Kept separate — SecretMoney vs. SecretInvoice: general-ledger accounting (QuickBooks) versus dedicated invoicing and payment tracking (FreshBooks) are different paid products many teams run side by side.

Kept separate — SecretWiki vs. SecretKnowledge: internal team documentation versus customer-facing published help content (GitBook) are different use cases and different products.

Kept separate — SecretDrive vs. SecretVault: general file sync (Dropbox Plus) versus access-controlled, audit-logged sharing (Dropbox Business Advanced) is a materially different, separately priced feature tier.
