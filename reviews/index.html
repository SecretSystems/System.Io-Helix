How It Works

Three Steps.
Zero Friction.

01
Set it on the counter
The card stands on its own — checkout counter, table, front desk, wherever customers naturally look when they're happy with the service.
02
Customer taps or scans
Any modern phone taps the card (NFC) or scans the printed QR code. No app required — it just opens their browser.
03
They land on your review page
Their phone opens straight to your Google Business review screen, star rating ready to tap. Fewer steps means more reviews actually get left.

Why It Works

Every Extra Step
Loses a Review.

Most happy customers mean to leave a review and never do — because they'd have to open Google, search your business name, find the right listing, and then find the review button. That's four steps too many. A tap-to-review card removes all of them.

  • Custom-branded with your business name and colors
  • Durable countertop stand — no batteries, no charging, no app
  • NFC tap works on iPhone (iOS 14+) and nearly all modern Android phones
  • Printed QR code as a fallback for any phone, any age
  • Links directly to your Google Business review page — we set it up for you
  • Can be reprogrammed if your review link or business info changes
