The Big Idea

TV Advertising Without Advertising to Everyone

Traditional TV buys a program or a time slot and hopes the right people are watching. Connected TV lets a campaign define the audience it wants to reach — and, depending on the platform, deliver ads across eligible streaming inventory to that audience instead.

Traditional TV

Buy a time slot. Hope the right people are watching.
  • Broad market/program reach — geographic market, not a defined audience
  • Limited control over who actually sees the ad
  • Attribution is hard — a customer has to mention it
  • Often requires a larger, less flexible buying commitment

Connected TV

Define the audience. Reach eligible viewers across streaming inventory.
  • ZIP code, city, DMA, or geographic-area targeting where supported
  • Age, gender, household income, interests & behaviors where supported
  • In-market audiences, content categories, first-party & lookalike audiences where supported
  • Previous website visitors / retargeting where supported

Website Retargeting

Your Website Visitor Leaves.
Your Advertising Doesn't Have To.

Someone visits your website and leaves without converting. On platforms that support it, that visit can make them eligible for a retargeting audience your streaming commercial may later reach — reinforcing your brand on the biggest screen in the house instead of letting the visit go to waste.

This uses privacy-safe audience matching, not individual visitor identification — platforms apply minimum audience-size thresholds and their own eligibility rules, so not every visitor is guaranteed to see the ad.
